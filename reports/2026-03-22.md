## 🔴 Urgent Actions (do this week)

**1. Fix the mass split-traffic problem across all diffuser/oil content**

- **Problem:** At least **six separate Āhuru URLs** are appearing in Google search results for the same "essential oils for diffuser / oils for diffuser / diffuser oil / diffuser oils" queries. Together these queries generated over **16,000 impressions in the last ~90 days (Search Console)**, yet produced fewer than 40 clicks combined across the competing pages — because Google cannot decide which page to promote. Your guide (`/blogs/guide/best-essential-oils-for-diffusers-guide`) already holds positions 4–7 and earns the vast majority of those impressions; the collection and blog pages are just cannibalising (splitting) its ranking power.
- **Fix:**
  1. Open Shopify → Online Store → Navigation. Ensure the only diffuser-oil page linked prominently in your main navigation and footer is `/blogs/guide/best-essential-oils-for-diffusers-guide` (for informational searches) and `/collections/essential-oils-for-diffuser` (for shopping intent). Remove or de-link the others from navigation.
  2. Go to each of the following weaker pages and add a sentence at the top with a clear internal link to the guide: `/blogs/guide/essential-oil-in-diffuser`, `/blogs/diffuser/best-diffuser-oils-nz`, `/blogs/guide/diffuser-oil`, `/blogs/diffuser-oil/diffuser-oil-nz-for-respiratory-health`.
  3. In Shopify, update the SEO title of `/collections/essential-oils-for-diffuser` so it targets **shopping** intent, not informational: change it to `Essential Oils for Diffusers NZ — Shop Online | Āhuru` (targets people ready to buy, not people researching).
  4. For any of those weaker blog posts that duplicate the guide's content closely, consider adding the HTML tag `<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.ahurucandles.co.nz/blogs/guide/best-essential-oils-for-diffusers-guide">` (a canonical tag tells Google "treat this other page as the main version") — your developer can add this in the blog post template in Shopify.
- **Why now:** This is your highest-impression page by a massive margin — 199,728 impressions in the last ~90 days (Search Console) but only a 0.33% CTR (click-through rate). Fixing the split is the single highest-leverage action on the entire site right now.

## ⚠️ Same keyword, multiple pages (splitting traffic)

**What this means (for Ting):** Google is showing **more than one** of Āhuru's URLs for the **same search phrase**. Those pages **share** impressions and clicks, so none of them ranks as strongly as a single focused page could. It is not "bad luck"; it is usually fixable by choosing **one main page** for that topic and **de-emphasising** the others (merge content, tighten internal links, adjust titles so each page targets a *different* intent, or redirect if two URLs say the same thing).
